============================================================
PUBLIC RELEASE STATUS
============================================================

The .NET and NuGet license and notice inventory has been completed for the
verified YokeForge 0.1.0 candidate.

PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION REMAINS BLOCKED ONLY BY THE TWO UNRESOLVED ITEMS IDENTIFIED
BELOW:

1. Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 native SimConnect.dll redistribution rights.
2. Microsoft.WindowsAppSDK.WinUI 2.3.x stable-package license conflict.

Do not publish YokeForge.exe or an installer until both matters have been
resolved or the affected component has been replaced by a legally verified
distribution method.

============================================================
1. MICROSOFT FLIGHT SIMULATOR 2024 SIMCONNECT
============================================================

YokeForge communicates with Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 through the
SimConnect API.

The verified candidate embeds the unmodified native SimConnect.dll in its
self-contained application bundle.

Microsoft Flight Simulator, SimConnect, Microsoft and Windows are trademarks
or registered trademarks of Microsoft Corporation.

YokeForge is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or
supported by Microsoft Corporation.

STATUS: PENDING BEFORE PUBLIC RELEASE

An official redistribution clarification has been requested at:

https://devsupport.flightsimulator.com/t/redistribution-rights-for-msfs-2024-native-simconnect-dll/18233

The public release requires confirmation of:

- whether the unmodified native SimConnect.dll may be redistributed with an
  external application;
- whether it may be embedded unchanged inside a self-contained executable;
- which Microsoft copyright, license and attribution notices are required;
- whether SimConnect.msi must be used instead.

No SimConnect redistribution right is claimed by this document.
